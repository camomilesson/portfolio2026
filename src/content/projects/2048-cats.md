## A familiar puzzle, with cats

2048 Cats is a mobile-first Unity game that combines the familiar sliding-tile puzzle with cat-themed artwork, animated feedback, and additional ways to recover a crowded board.

## A little more strategy

- **Undo** takes back an action.
- **Kill** removes a selected tile.
- **Nuke** halves tile values and removes tiles worth 2.

Merging milestone tiles earns power-up charges. The game accounts for available rescue power-ups when checking whether play can continue.

## The details around the board

Swipe and keyboard controls, buffered movement input, tile and power-up animations, music, and sound effects shape the experience. An animated cat wanders using a NavMesh agent.

Saved games, persistent statistics, audio settings, and confirmation dialogs round out the game. Core board and move logic are separated from Unity views, persistence, statistics, and audio.
