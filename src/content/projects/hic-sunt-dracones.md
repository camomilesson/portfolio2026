## The idea

“Hic Sunt Dracones” means “Here be dragons.” This Android app gives that old map inscription a new purpose: the places you haven’t explored are hidden beneath a fog of war.

As you move, H3 hexagonal cells clear to reveal the map. Exploration is stored locally, so the map remembers where you’ve been.

## What it does

- Tracks movement to reveal new areas.
- Offers Default, Parchment, and Night map themes.
- Stores exploration data with Room.
- Supports background exploration through foreground tracking and a persistent notification.

## Built for Android

The project uses Kotlin, Google Maps, and H3 cells. It began as a Mobile Development course project at Harbour.Space.
